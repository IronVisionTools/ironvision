VYRON AgentOS

An automation system that takes over repetitive work. Agents scan the inbox, produce a daily summary, take and verify backups, and track cost. You watch it all from one panel — or send a command from Telegram.

  • Watchdogs: disk, errors, delays — it tells you the moment something goes wrong.
  • Scheduled jobs: a 7am summary every morning, a nightly backup, a monthly report.
  • Telegram: type "give me today's summary" and the task shows up in the panel.
  • Browser automation: fills forms, pulls data, takes screenshots.
  • Set up for your business specifically, 2-4 weeks to deploy, your data stays with you.
  • Automatic archiving: downloads YouTube channels and websites with their transcripts and images, making them searchable in natural language.
  • Legal-watch bot: scans the Official Gazette every morning and summarizes changes relevant to your industry.
  • Watchdogs on duty: monitors your site, server and forms 24/7, alerts you the moment something crashes, and takes the first response itself.
  • Appointment and email lines: reads incoming messages, matches them against your calendar, and replies on your behalf.
  • Backup verification: automatically opens and tests backups it takes, making sure they aren't corrupted.

Sesli Şef (Voice Chief)

This is not a speech-to-text dictation tool: you have a two-way conversation with your terminal-based AI agent (Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex) — you speak, the agent answers out loud. Before long you're working by listening and talking without ever looking at the screen.

  • Most tools only handle dictation — here the answer talks back too.
  • Audio never leaves this server — speech-to-text runs locally.
  • You can also type instead of using the microphone.
  • Gives your eyes a rest during long sessions, without losing your place.

About

IRON VISION

IRON VISION is a software startup based in Türkiye. We build AI tools that take on the repetitive load of running a business: we do the setup, the product runs on your side, your data stays with you. We're growing with support from AWS's startup program.

We use our own products before we sell them. Inside AgentOS, more than 10 micro-apps run today: watchdogs, a backup verifier, a voice bridge, email and appointment pipelines, a site-production line, a semantic search engine. We don't recommend a tool to a client that doesn't work for us.

Where we're headed is clear: a business assistant that runs itself. Each of these micro-apps is turning into a separate product over time. We've had support from AWS's startup program and are applying for the next tier. Our door is open for partnerships and support.
