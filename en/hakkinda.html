About

IRON VISION

A software startup based in Türkiye. From small shops to bigger companies, freelancers to teams — we build AI tools that lighten the daily load.

Why we exist

IRON VISION is a software startup based in Türkiye. We build AI tools that take on the repetitive load of running a business: we do the setup, the product runs on your side, your data stays with you. We're growing with support from AWS's startup program.

Running a business today increasingly means managing the processes behind the work rather than the work itself. A hotel owner should spend their time with guests, not checking whether the reservation system has crashed. A manager should be developing strategy, not losing hours digging through old meeting notes for one piece of information. We exist to remove that invisible load. We see AI not as a technology showcase but as a colleague working quietly in the background, one that doesn't get tired and doesn't make mistakes. Our goal is to automate your digital operations so you're free to focus on the actual work.

What we do

We build digital tools that take over the tasks businesses look at every day and think "I wish someone else would just do this." We hold one rule without exception while doing it: "We handle setup, the product runs on your side, your data stays with you."

A lot of software pulls your data into its own center and makes you dependent on it. We do the opposite. We install the tools we build into your system and hand them over running. Every bit of data the tool produces and processes stays entirely under your control. We're turning AI from an outsourced service into an assistant that works directly under your business's own ownership.

How we work: we try it on ourselves first

Software that looks great on paper doesn't mean it'll work in the real world. That's why we've held one production philosophy since we started out in spring 2026: a tool that doesn't work for us doesn't get recommended to a client.

Every AI agent, every micro-app we build, we use in our own internal operations first. Our watchdog system protects our own digital infrastructure before anyone else's. We use Sesli Şef in our own daily work first: we give commands by voice and hand tasks off to agents. Along the way we find the gaps, fix the rough edges, and smooth the tool out. Every product we offer you has already passed the stress test of a real working environment.

Our products

As of today, here are the active tools we offer businesses:

  • VyroN Agents OS — Our main backbone, letting you manage all of your business's AI agents and micro-apps from a single panel. More than 10 apps run inside it today, including watchdogs that monitor systems 24/7, backup verifiers that protect data safety, voice bridges, email and appointment lines that take over customer communication, and a semantic search engine that lets you search internal documents in natural language.
    • Automatic archiving: downloads given YouTube channels and websites with their transcripts and images, turning them into a company library searchable in natural language.
    • Legal-watch bot: scans the Official Gazette every morning and brings you a summary of changes relevant to your industry.
    • Watchdogs on duty: checks whether your digital systems (site, server, forms) are up 24/7, alerts you the moment something crashes, and takes the first response itself.
    • Appointment and email lines: reads incoming customer messages, understands the context, matches them against your calendar, and replies professionally on your behalf.
    • Backup verification: automatically opens and tests your regularly taken system backups, making sure the data isn't corrupted.
  • Sesli Şef — The assistant that ends the need to be at your computer. From your phone, using only your voice, give instructions to your AI sessions, listen to reports and get work done.
  • MoyMote — Control your external monitor's brightness, blue light and power from your phone over a local or secure private network. Pairs in seconds with a QR code. Available on the Microsoft Store.
  • Social media automation — A system that runs your business's social media for you. You just give it a topic; the tool writes a script from it, records a voiceover, edits the video, and publishes it on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube along with the caption. It produces anything from short vertical videos to 40-minute long-form content. We adapt the ready-made system to your brand and manage the account connections and the initial warm-up period.
  • Try-On — Coming soon. A "try it on" button for boutiques and small e-commerce sites: shoppers see the product on their own photo before buying. In development.

Where we're headed

Our vision is clear: to create a "business assistant that runs itself." Every micro-app running under VyroN Agents OS today is turning, over time, into a strong, independent product that can stand on its own. We keep automating businesses' digital infrastructure piece by piece, building flawless background systems that run with zero human intervention.
