I started Little Wren because I kept staying in places where nobody knew my name by the second morning. Here you will be known by the first.

Good to know

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out by 11 am. Arriving earlier or later? Tell us in the form and we will make it work.

Do you accept pets?

Please ask us before booking. Some rooms suit a well-behaved dog better than others, and we would rather agree it in advance.

Is there parking?

Yes, a few free spaces on site. Tell us if you are arriving by car so we can keep one for you.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please ask us. We will explain the conditions for your dates in our reply, before you commit to anything.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Usually, yes. Mention allergies or preferences in the form and we will plan your breakfast around them.
