Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

In short: the website (ironvisiontools.com) does not track you; there is no analytics and no advertising. Questions you type into the VyroN Agents OS live demo are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate answers. Demo data is synthetic and temporary. Local tools such as MoyMote send no data.

Website (ironvisiontools.com)

The site uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage (localStorage) may hold only the interface language you chose; it never leaves your device. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

VyroN Agents OS live demo

  • Anthropic Claude API: Text you type into Q&A fields in the demo, such as "Ask the Chief", is sent to Anthropic's Claude API (Anthropic, PBC) to produce the answer. Please do not enter personal or confidential information. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Session cookie: Guest sign-in uses a technical (strictly necessary) session cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising or tracking and expires when the session ends.
  • Synthetic, temporary data: Tasks, emails, invoices and sheets in the demo are fictional, not real person or company data. Actions you take as a guest (approve, reset, etc.) are for demonstration and temporary; "Reset demo" restores them.
  • Scripted sections: Model-routing traces, invoice/email tool calls and Voice Chief scenarios are pre-written examples; no real email or post is sent and no microphone is used.
  • Rate limit: The number of questions per hour is limited to prevent abuse, using technical connection/session information.

Our products

MoyMote and similar local tools run on your device and send no usage data, personal data or content. In Voice Chief, speech recognition runs locally. For VyroN Agents OS and other automations installed for a customer, data stays in the customer's own environment; which AI provider is used is agreed with the customer in writing before installation.

Your rights (Turkish KVKK)

Changes
